The Menu

Slow-cooked, charcoal-grilled, freshly pounded. Spice levels run from 🌶️ mild to 🌶️🌶️🌶️ fiery.

From a family kitchen in Padang

Warung Indah began with our grandmother's rendang, cooked down over six hours in a clay pot in West Sumatra. Everything we serve still starts from that same spice paste — galangal, lemongrass, candlenut and turmeric, pounded by hand each morning.

In 2016 we brought those recipes to Brussels, and we haven't changed a thing since. Same family, same wok, same obsession with getting the sambal right.

— The Hartono family
